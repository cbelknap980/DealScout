Checks · the standard every search follows

Nothing counts until the ad itself has been read, and the cheaper it looks the harder it's checked.

Every search runs the same sequence, whatever the item. Search results, alert emails and pasted links only point at an ad; the ad is what gets checked.

01

Read the ad itself

Every listing is opened before it's shown: price, condition, title or paperwork, seller and availability come from the live ad. Until then it's marked unverified.

02

Keep the price current

Open listings are reopened at least twice a day. Price cuts, sold ads and changed details are recorded, and a deal needs its price confirmed within 24 hours.

03

Photos and red flags

Anything priced like a deal gets every photo reviewed, a scan for scam language, a search for the same ad elsewhere, and a VIN or serial check against the item's own format.

04

A second look when it's very cheap

35% or more under, or 25% under from a private seller: an independent review that hasn't seen the first verdict, then the ad is reopened. Both have to pass.

05

Once more before any email

Right before a deal email goes out, each ad is reopened to confirm it's still listed at that price. Sold or changed ads are dropped or re-scored.
